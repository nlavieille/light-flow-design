Privacy Policy

Short version: we use your information to reply to you and to help you book a call. We do not sell your data, and we are not weird about this.

Last updated: 9 September 2026

If you filled out a Facebook or Meta lead form, this is the policy that covers it. It is written to be readable by humans, not only by lawyers.

Information we collect

When you contact us or submit a lead form, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business name and business type
  • Any details you voluntarily share about your needs

We only collect information you choose to share with us.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, schedule and conduct discovery calls, provide information about our systems, marketing and content services, and follow up regarding your request.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Communication

By submitting your information, you agree that we may contact you by email, phone, or text message regarding your enquiry.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to text messages, or by emailing us to request removal.

Data storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information. Your data may be stored in systems we use to manage enquiries — for example email, scheduling tools, CRM systems, or automation platforms.

Access is limited to Princess Light Media and the trusted service providers necessary to operate our business.

Third-party services

We may use third-party services (such as Facebook/Meta, scheduling tools, email platforms, or automation software) to collect and manage enquiries. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data.

Your rights

You may request to access the personal information we hold about you, correct or update it, or request its deletion. To do so, contact us using the details below.
