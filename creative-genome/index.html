01 — The idea

Most brands do not need “more content.” They need cleaner tests.

Paid social rewards creative volume, but random volume burns time. Creative Genome turns a product page into a structured experiment: each asset tests a specific angle, hook, format or proof point.

Problem

Winners are rare

Most creatives will not become outliers. The job is to create enough useful variation to find signal without flooding the account with random assets.

Method

Angle × hook × format

Every concept is mapped before it is produced, so the brand knows what each ad is trying to learn.

Guardrail

Claims stay disciplined

Beauty creative can move fast without inventing testimonials, medical claims or risky before-and-after promises.

03 — Creative Genome

Every concept gets a reason to exist.

Before anything is produced, each idea is scored against the creative DNA that matters for paid social.

01

Hook clarity

Can someone understand the point in the first two seconds?

02

Brand fit

Does it feel native to the brand, or like a generic AI ad wearing the brand’s colours?

03

Testing value

If this wins or loses, will the team learn something useful?

04

Product proof

Is the product visible, clear and connected to a believable customer problem?

05

Claims safety

Does the script avoid fake testimonials, unsupported claims and risky transformation language?

06

Production feasibility

Can it be made quickly with available product assets, AI-assisted tools, or creator footage?

05 — How the week runs

A sprint, not a six-week agency process.

Day 1

Brief from the product page

We read the product page, founder story, current public creative and claims language, then build the first message map.

Days 2–3

Angles and hooks

We create the test matrix: customer problem, proof point, angle, hook, first-frame idea and format.

Days 4–5

Scripts and mockups

UGC-style scripts, storyboard concepts, statics and rough spec visuals are created and scored.

Day 6

QC and claims review

Concepts are cleaned up for brand fit, clarity and compliance before delivery.

Day 7

Launch matrix

You receive the pack organized by angle × hook × format, with notes on what to test first.

Next

Keep or continue

Use the pack internally, hand it to creators, or continue into monthly creative testing.

FAQ

Questions before the first sprint

Is this an agency retainer?

No. The first offer is a fixed 7-day sprint. If the pack is useful, it can become monthly creative testing, but the pilot stands alone.

Do you guarantee ad performance?

No. We do not promise ROAS. We guarantee a structured creative test pack with clear hooks, angles, scripts, compliance notes and a launch matrix.

Do you use AI?

Yes. Creative Genome is AI-assisted and human-directed. Strategy, taste, claims discipline and final judgment stay human-led.

Will you copy competitor ads?

No. We benchmark category patterns, then create original concepts around your own product, brand, story and approved claims.

What do you need from us?

A product URL is enough to start. Better inputs include approved claims, product photos, brand examples, existing best/worst ads and any customer language you have rights to use.
