Most local service businesses do not have a marketing problem. They have an interruption problem. The phone rings during a colour service. The DM arrives at midnight. The client who forgot their appointment leaves a hundred-dollar hole in the day. None of that is fixed by posting more.

Admin-Busters builds the systems that absorb the interruptions — booking, reminders, follow-up, reviews, and the Google listing that decides whether anyone finds you at all — and then runs them, so they keep working when you get busy.

We build practical systems for businesses that don’t have a Chief Innovation Officer.

FAQ

What people ask before they call

What is Admin-Busters?

The local-business half of Princess Light Media. We set up — and then run — the marketing and admin systems a service business needs: online booking, reminders, follow-up, reviews, Google Business Profile, social content and video ads.

Not a fancy agency built for Fortune 500 companies. Real work for real businesses.

Which businesses do you work with?

Hair salons and barbershops, nail salons, tattoo studios, med spas and aesthetic clinics, massage therapy clinics, gyms and fitness studios — including yoga, pilates, boxing and martial arts — and dog groomers.

If your trade is not listed, the underlying problems are usually the same. Ask.

What areas do you cover?

Surrey, Langley, Delta, White Rock, Burnaby, Richmond, Coquitlam, New Westminster and Vancouver. Based in Surrey.

Do I have to change the software I already use?

Usually not. If your booking platform works and your staff know it, we build around it. Replacing software people have already learned is a last resort.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No — and treat anyone who does with suspicion. What we commit to is a complete, correctly configured, actively maintained presence. That is the part that is actually within anyone’s control.

What does the first call involve?

Fifteen minutes, free, no pitch deck. We look at what’s messy, repetitive or leaking money. Most calls end with two or three things you could go and fix yourself, whether or not you ever hire us.
