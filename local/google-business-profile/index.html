Package 1

The one-time fix, step by step

Step 1

Claim and verify your listing

You cannot edit what you do not own. We make sure you control the profile before anyone else edits it — including Google quietly applying suggestions submitted by strangers.

Step 2

Fix your name, address and phone

These three have to match exactly everywhere your business appears online. Mismatches as small as “St” versus “Street” actively hurt your ranking. We find them and fix them at the source.

Step 3

Set the right categories

The single biggest lever for showing up in the right searches. We choose the closest primary category and add every relevant secondary category — the ones most businesses never touch.

Step 4

Fill out every field

Hours, holiday hours, service area, payment methods, service list, attributes. Google rewards completeness, and a full profile answers the customer’s question before they even click.

Step 5

Add real photos

Exterior, interior, team and your actual work. Listings with photos get meaningfully more calls and direction requests. This is usually the most visible gap and the fastest win.

Package 2

Keeping it working

A clean listing gets you in the game. Staying active keeps you there. Google rewards businesses that show up consistently — and customers notice when you reply.

Step 6

Review generation and responses

A QR code at your counter, a text after each job, and a response to every review — good or bad. This is the step that compounds. Same star rating as your competitor, but you reply and they don’t. Customers notice.

Step 7

Weekly Google posts

Updates, offers, before-and-afters, posted weekly to your profile. Most local businesses have never used this feature. The ones that do read as the current, active choice.

Step 8

Questions and answers monitoring

Anyone can answer questions on your listing, including competitors with wrong information. We seed it with the questions customers actually ask and monitor it so bad answers don’t sit there unchallenged.

FAQ

Google Business Profile questions

What is Google Business Profile optimisation?

It is the work of claiming, correcting and completing the free listing that shows up in Google Search and Google Maps — then keeping it active with photos, weekly posts, review responses and answered questions.

It is the highest-return marketing work available to most local businesses, and it costs nothing but attention.

Why does an incomplete Google listing hurt my business?

Because Google favours complete, active listings when someone searches nearby. Missing hours, no photos, wrong categories, no recent activity — you simply don’t make the shortlist, and the person calls whoever did.

It has nothing to do with how good your work is. That is the frustrating part, and also the fixable part.

How long does it take to fix a Google Business Profile?

The cleanup itself takes days once verification comes through — Google’s verification step is the unpredictable part. Ranking movement from ongoing activity builds over weeks and months, not overnight. Anyone promising otherwise is guessing.

Do you guarantee first-page Google Maps rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a ranking. What we guarantee is a complete, correct, actively maintained listing — the part that is genuinely within anyone’s control, and the part almost every competitor gets wrong.
