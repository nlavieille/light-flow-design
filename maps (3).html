Package 1 — The One-Time Fix
Step 1 Claim & Verify Your Listing

You can't edit what you don't own. We make sure you control the profile before anyone else edits it — including Google applying random suggestions from strangers.

Step 2 Fix Your Name, Address & Phone

These three must match exactly everywhere your business appears online. Mismatches — even small ones like "St" vs "Street" — actively hurt your ranking. We find them and fix them at the source.

Step 3 Set the Right Categories

The single biggest lever for showing up in the right searches. We pick the closest primary category and add every relevant secondary category — the ones most businesses miss entirely.

Step 4 Fill Out Every Field

Hours, holiday hours, service area, payment methods, services list, attributes. Google rewards completeness. A fully filled profile answers the customer's question before they even click.

Step 5 Add Real Photos

Exterior, interior, team, and your actual work. Listings with photos get significantly more calls and direction requests. This is usually the fastest visible gap — and the easiest win.

Package 2 — Keep It Working (Monthly)
A clean listing gets you in the game. Staying active keeps you there. Google rewards businesses that show up consistently — and customers notice when you respond.
Step 6 Review Generation & Responses

A QR code at your counter, a text after each job, and a response to every review — good or bad. This is the step that compounds. Same star rating as your competitor, but you respond and they don't. Customers choose you.

Step 7 Weekly Google Posts

Updates, offers, before-and-afters — posted weekly to your profile. Most local businesses have never touched this feature. The ones that do show up as the active, current choice.

Step 8 Q&A Monitoring

Anyone can answer questions on your listing — including competitors with wrong information. We seed it with the questions customers actually ask, and monitor it so bad answers don't sit there unchallenged.
