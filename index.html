How projects work

The brief
A paragraph on what the product is and who it's for. If you don't have one, a fifteen-minute call produces it.
Starting point
Existing product photography, a logo and a colour palette. No shoot required.
Turnaround
Days rather than weeks. The whole asset set comes out of a single production pass.
Pricing
Fixed price quoted per brief, agreed before anything starts. No hourly, no retainer.
How it's made
AI-native production. Generative video and motion tools, directed and finished by a person. Disclosed to every client.
