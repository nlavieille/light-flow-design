FAQ

How this works, in practice

How are video projects priced?

Per brief, fixed, agreed up front. The two variables are how many finished assets you need and how much visual world has to be built to make them hang together.

No hourly. No retainer. No change-order theatre.

What do I need to send you to start?

Three things: a paragraph on what the product is and who it's for, whatever product photography you already have, and your logo and colours.

If you don't have a brief, a fifteen-minute call produces one.

How many revisions are included?

One full revision pass after first delivery is included. Because assets come out of a defined visual world rather than a filmed shoot, a change to a hook or a cut is hours of work, not another shoot day.

Do you handle posting and paid media?

Delivery includes captions, hooks and a suggested posting order, so the assets are usable the day they land.

If you want someone to actually run the posting, reviews and local search, that lives in Admin-Busters.

Is the work AI-generated?

Yes, and it's the point. The studio is AI-native — generative video and motion tools, directed, art-directed and finished by a person.

That combination is the only reason eleven finished assets can cost roughly what one shoot day used to. We don't hide it, and clients are told exactly how their work is made.
