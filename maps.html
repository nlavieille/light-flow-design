Phase 1 — The One-Time Cleanup (Steps 1–5)
Step 1 Claim & Verify Your Listing

Nothing else matters until you own the profile. If it's unclaimed, anyone can suggest edits — and Google will apply them. Verification is usually a postcard (5–7 days) or instant by phone for some business types. If a previous owner or agency claimed it, we request an ownership transfer. We make sure you're the one in control before touching anything else.

Step 2 Fix Your Name, Address & Phone

Your business name, address, and phone number must match exactly across Google, your website, Facebook, Yelp, and any directories where you appear. Small differences — "St" vs "Street," an old number still floating around somewhere — actively hurt your local ranking. We track down the mismatches and clean them up at the source.

Step 3 Pick the Right Categories

This is the single biggest lever for showing up in the right searches. One primary category (the closest match to what you actually do) plus up to nine relevant secondary categories. A nail salon should have "Nail Salon" as primary, then add "Eyelash Salon," "Waxing Hair Removal Service," and others that apply. Wrong or too-generic categories are a silent ranking killer most businesses never fix.

Step 4 Fill Out Every Field

Hours (including holiday hours), service area, payment methods, accessibility features, services list with descriptions, and any applicable attributes like "women-owned" or "veteran-owned." Google rewards completeness — profiles with more filled fields rank better and convert better, because they answer the searcher's question before they even click through to your website.

Step 5 Upload Real, Recent Photos

Listings with photos get significantly more calls and direction requests than those without. At minimum: an exterior shot so people can find you in person, an interior, a team or owner photo, and 5–10 of your actual work or product. Photos are often the fastest visible win — and the gap most local businesses haven't noticed they have.

✂ ✂ ✂
Phase 2 — Ongoing Monthly (Steps 6–8)
A cleaned-up listing is the foundation. But Google rewards businesses that stay active. Steps 6–8 keep your listing fresh, your reviews answered, and your ranking from sliding.
Step 6 Review Generation & Responses

Not just randomly asking for reviews — a repeatable process: a QR code at your counter linking directly to your review page, a text sent after each appointment or job, and a short script your team can use. Every existing review, good or bad, gets a thoughtful professional response. This is the step that compounds over time and separates you from a competitor with the same star rating who never replies.

Step 7 Weekly Google Posts

Google lets businesses post updates, offers, and events directly to the profile — similar to a social feed that lives right on your listing. A weekly post (a promo, a new arrival, a before-and-after) signals an active business to Google's algorithm and gives searchers a reason to choose you over an equally-rated competitor who hasn't posted in six months. Most owner-operated businesses have never touched this feature.

Step 8 Q&A Management

Anyone can post a question on your Google listing — and anyone can answer it, including competitors with wrong information. We seed the Q&A section with the 3–5 questions customers actually ask (parking, walk-ins vs. appointment, payment methods, etc.) and answer them as the owner. Then we monitor for new questions before bad answers sit there unchallenged.
