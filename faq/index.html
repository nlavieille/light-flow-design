FAQ

The studio, the work, and the local side

What is Princess Light Media?

An AI-native creative studio in Surrey, British Columbia, founded by Noel Lavieille. Two service lines: motion design video ads for product brands, and done-for-you local marketing for service businesses under the Admin-Busters name.

Who is Noel Lavieille?

The founder, and the person who does the work. Roughly twenty years in data architecture and business intelligence, most recently at VP level at State Street, before starting the studio. Longer version here.

How fast can a video ad be delivered?

Days rather than weeks. There is no shoot day to schedule and no edit suite to book — the whole asset set comes out of one production pass.

Do you need to film my product?

No. Existing product photography, a logo and a colour palette are usually enough. The Luppolo Brewing campaign was built from one photo.

What does a video project cost?

Fixed price, quoted per brief, agreed before anything starts. The variables are how many finished assets you need and how much visual world has to be built. No hourly, no retainer.

Is the video work AI-generated?

Yes, and every client is told so. Generative video and motion tools, directed, art-directed and finished by a person. It's the specific reason a small brand can afford cinematic-quality video at all.

Is the work on the site real client work?

Both, clearly labelled. Spec pieces are produced independently to show the approach and are marked as spec on the work page. No invented client list.

What is Admin-Busters?

The local-business half of the studio. Booking and reminders, follow-up, reviews, Google Business Profile, social content and local search — set up and run for you. Details here.

Which local industries do you work with?

Hair salons and barbershops, nail salons, tattoo studios, med spas and aesthetic clinics, massage therapy clinics, gyms and fitness studios, and dog groomers. Every industry, every problem, every fix.

What areas do you serve?

Local marketing: Surrey, Langley, Delta, White Rock, Burnaby, Richmond, Coquitlam, New Westminster and Vancouver.

Video work: anywhere. The brief arrives by email and so does the campaign.

Can you fix my Google Business Profile?

Yes — a one-time cleanup, or ongoing management. The full eight-step breakdown is here, including a free fifteen-minute audit of your actual listing.

Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. What's promised is a complete, correctly configured, actively maintained presence — which is the part anyone can actually control.

How do I get started?

Email hello@princesslight.com with a paragraph, or call 778-238-0206. Usually a same-day reply.

Question not answered?

Ask it directly. You'll get a straight answer from the person who does the work.
