The Brief

Most singles disappear before they're heard.

The label drops a Reel on Friday. The artist tags two friends. By Monday, the algorithm has moved on, the song has eight thousand streams, and the rollout that was meant to last two weeks lasts about thirty-six hours.

Slow Burn is a spec case study built to solve that. One Friday release date. One artist. One song. Eleven pieces of content sharing a single visual world, sequenced across fourteen days — each one engineered for a specific moment in the campaign.

The teaser asks a question. The cutdowns answer it. The UGC clip disappears into the algorithm on purpose. The Canvas loop lives inside Spotify itself. The story frames stitch the gaps. The quote cards punctuate.

Eleven posts. One production pass. Two weeks of content from a single visual brief.

Kaira is fictional. The pack is real — produced in approximately one production week using a Nano Banana Pro / Veo 3.1 / Kling 2.1 + 2.6 / CapCut pipeline. The point is not the artist. The point is the system.

What does a single creative pass deliver when the visual language is locked from the cover art outward?

The Artist

Kaira.

Records in her bedroom. Produces everything herself. Performs live three or four times a year — and that scarcity is part of the brand.

Her songs do not shout. They smoulder. Slow Burn is the lead single off her forthcoming EP Late Bloomer — alt-pop about taking too long to leave, recorded in a single sitting at four in the morning, never edited.

The Rollout

Fourteen days. Eleven posts. One song.

The pack is sequenced for a Friday release. Each asset has a job — and a moment. The system below assumes a label is supporting the rollout across the artist's owned channels.

  • T − 14 Teaser cutdown + Story frame 1 Reels · TikTok · Story
  • T − 10 Quote card · title lyric Feed · X
  • T − 7 Story frame 2 · countdown Story
  • T − 5 Quote card · artist voice Feed
  • T − 3 Pre-save cutdown + Story frame 3 Reels · TikTok · Story
  • T − 1 UGC creator clip TikTok · Reels
  • Day 0 Hero reel + Story frame 4 + Canvas live on Spotify Everywhere
  • T + 1 Release-day cutdown + third quote card Feed · Reels
  • T + 2 Story frame 5 · fan repost template Story
