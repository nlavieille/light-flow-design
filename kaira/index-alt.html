A Demonstration

What follows is a complete pack — every asset in the bundle — built for a fictional artist named KAIRA and her debut single, Slow Burn.

Kaira isn't real. The pack is. We built it to show what a single creative brief can deliver when the visual language is locked from the cover art outward.

The Artist

Meet Kaira.

Records in her bedroom in Mile End. Produces everything herself. Performs live three or four times a year — and that scarcity is part of the brand.

Her songs do not shout. They smoulder. Slow Burn is the lead single off her forthcoming EP Late Bloomer — alt-pop about taking too long to leave, recorded in a single sitting at four in the morning, never edited.

  • Project KAIRA · stylised all-caps, one word
  • Based Montreal · Mile End
  • Genre Alt-pop · bedroom electronic · lofi-adjacent
  • For Fans Of Clairo, Joji, Arlo Parks, early Rina Sawayama
  • Aesthetic Early-to-mid 20s · androgynous styling · soft but intentional
  • The Single Slow Burn · 3:14 · C♯ minor · 82 BPM
  • Release Friday, June 5, 2026
The Cover

Where the visual world begins.

Every other asset in the pack — the reels, the story frames, the quote cards, the Canvas — is built from this image. Lock the cover, lock the world.

Primary Cover

Kaira sits on the edge of an unmade bed in soft amber lamplight. She's wearing an oversized white t-shirt, shoulders bare. A lit cigarette rests in an ashtray on the nightstand beside her. The window across the room glows the specific blue of pre-dawn. Her hair is just-slept-on.

The framing is intimate but not sexualised — it reads as aftermath, not seduction.

Title treatment: "SLOW BURN" in a thin serif, set very small in the bottom-left. Artist name "KAIRA" in the bottom-right, even smaller. Everything breathes. The image does the work, not the type.

Alt Cover · Still Life

The same nightstand, shot top-down. Just the ashtray, the cigarette, one earring, a water glass. No subject, no figure — only the objects she left behind.

Used as the source frame for the Spotify Canvas loop, and as the cover variant for merch and B-side feed posts.

Mood & Sonic Direction

The slow realisation that something is over.

Not heartbroken-crying. Heartbroken-smoking-a-cigarette-on-a-balcony-at-2am. Dry-eyed, clear-eyed, a little bit relieved.

The sound

A warm, slightly saturated drum machine. Detuned Rhodes-style keys. A finger-picked nylon guitar panned right. Layered breathy vocals with a doubled whisper track underneath.

No big drop. No build-and-release. A steady smoulder that resolves, not explodes.
