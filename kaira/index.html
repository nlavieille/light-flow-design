The Brief

Most single rollouts collapse on day three.

The label drops a Reel, the artist tags two friends, and momentum dies before the song finds its own playlist. Slow Burn is a spec case study built to solve that — one Friday release date, one fictional artist, one song, eleven pieces of content sharing a single visual world and unfolding across two weeks.

Each asset is engineered for a specific moment in the campaign: the teaser that asks a question, the cutdown that pushes the pre-save, the UGC clip that disappears into the algorithm, the Canvas loop that lives inside Spotify itself. Eleven posts from one production pass. Two weeks of content from a single visual brief.

Kaira is fictional. The pack is real — produced in approximately one production week using a Nano Banana Pro / Veo 3.1 / Kling 2.6 / CapCut pipeline. The point is not the artist. The point is the system: what a single creative pass delivers when the visual language is locked from the cover art outward.
