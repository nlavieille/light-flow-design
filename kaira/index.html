Meet the artist
The Artist

Kaira.

Records in her bedroom. Produces everything herself. Performs live three or four times a year — and that scarcity is part of the brand.

Her songs do not shout. They smoulder. Slow Burn is the lead single off her forthcoming EP Late Bloomer — alt-pop about taking too long to leave, recorded in a single sitting at four in the morning, never edited.

  • Genre Alt-pop · bedroom electronic · lofi-adjacent
  • Based Montreal · Mile End
  • For Fans Of Clairo, Joji, Arlo Parks, early Rina Sawayama
  • The Single Slow Burn · 3:14 · C♯ minor · 82 BPM
  • Release Friday, June 5, 2026
Mood & Sonic Direction

The slow realisation that something is over.

Not heartbroken-crying. Heartbroken-smoking-a-cigarette-on-a-balcony-at-2am. Dry-eyed, clear-eyed, a little bit relieved.

The sound

A warm, slightly saturated drum machine. Detuned Rhodes-style keys. A finger-picked nylon guitar panned right. Layered breathy vocals with a doubled whisper track underneath.

No big drop. No build-and-release. A steady smoulder that resolves, not explodes.

The image

Kaira sits on the edge of an unmade bed in soft amber lamplight. Her back is to the camera. The window across the room glows the specific blue of pre-dawn. A lit cigarette rests in an ashtray on the nightstand. Her hair is just-slept-on. The framing is intimate but not sexualised — it reads as aftermath, not seduction.

The image does the work. The type breathes. The whole thing is built to feel like a still pulled from a film you haven't seen yet.
